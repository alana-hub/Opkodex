Privacy

SmartCare Rwanda privacy notice

This frontend prototype is designed to demonstrate patient registration and hospital discovery without storing registration data in browser local storage.

What information does the registration form request?

It requests patient/parent identity, contact, address, insurance, dependent, appointment and supporting-document information needed to demonstrate the workflow.

Does this prototype store my registration in the browser?

No. The frontend does not write registration payloads to localStorage. Demo mode only creates an onscreen confirmation reference.

What is required before real patient data is processed?

A real deployment should use a secure backend, authenticated access, private document storage, encryption, audit logging, retention controls, consent processes and applicable privacy/security review.
