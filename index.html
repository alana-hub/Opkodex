FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SmartCare Rwanda?

SmartCare Rwanda is a digital patient self-registration and hospital navigation experience designed to simplify the journey from registration to care.

Do I have to register before visiting?

Pre-registration is designed to make the visit easier. Final availability and confirmation depend on the participating healthcare facility.

How does the helpdesk confirmation work?

The workflow provides for an automated email confirmation followed by a verification/support call from the Opkode X Helpdesk team.

Can I register a dependent?

Yes. The intake form supports children and other eligible dependents.

Is my information stored in this demo website?

No. The frontend demo does not persist sensitive registration data in browser storage. A real deployment should send data only to a secured backend.
